Family Dinner Protocol

From Request to Dinner in 6 Steps

1

"Dinner for the family tonight"

Chef receives your request and kicks off the pipeline.

2

Diet fetches profiles

Queries the database for family preferences, allergies, and nutritional targets.

3

Recipe finds meals & gaps

Searches Spoonacular for matching recipes and identifies missing ingredients.

4

Shopper prices on Swiggy

Checks Instamart availability and pricing for the missing items.

5

Budget calculates savings

Computes how much you save by cooking at home vs ordering delivery.

6

You approve → order executes

"Missing 2 items. Savings: ₹450. Order?" — Only after your "Yes" does the cart execute.
